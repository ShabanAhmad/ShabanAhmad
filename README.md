## 🧬 About Me

I'm a computational biologist at the **[Department of Plant and Environmental Sciences (PLEN)](https://plen.ku.dk/english/), [University of Copenhagen](https://www.ku.dk/en)**, where I build AI-driven pipelines that sit at the intersection of structural biology, machine learning, and environmental biotechnology. 

My journey spans from the bustling campus of **[Jamia Millia Islamia](https://www.jmi.ac.in/)** in New Delhi — where I completed my PhD developing deep-learning models for lung cancer drug discovery — through visiting stints in **[Finland](https://www.jyu.fi/en)**, to the North Sea shores of **Copenhagen**, where I currently chase some of the most persistent pollutants on Earth: **PFAS "forever chemicals"**. Along the way, I've accumulated over **100 publications**, an **H-index of 30**, **2,100+ citations**, and **650+ verified peer reviews** — but what I'm most proud of is the open-source tools and reproducible pipelines that anyone in the world can pick up and run.

I am actively seeking an **Assistant Professorship** to establish the **[Translational Computational Pharmacology (TCP) Group](https://shabanahmad.github.io/#research-vision)** — a lab where mechanism-aware AI meets real clinical and environmental challenges.

> **Note on contributions:** I commit both here (`ShabanAhmad`) and via my research group organisation **[KU-MGB](https://github.com/KU-MGB)** (Microbial Genomics and Biodegradation, University of Copenhagen). Both GitHub profiles represent my active scientific work — please explore both!
