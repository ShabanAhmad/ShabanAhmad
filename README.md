## About Me

I am a computational biologist at **[PLEN, University of Copenhagen](https://plen.ku.dk/english/)**, working on AI-driven pipelines that connect structural biology, machine learning, and environmental biotechnology.

My research path began at **[Jamia Millia Islamia](https://www.jmi.ac.in/)** in New Delhi, where I completed my PhD on deep-learning models for lung cancer drug discovery, followed by a visiting position at the **[University of Jyvaskyla, Finland](https://www.jyu.fi/en)**. I am now at Copenhagen working on PFAS "forever chemicals". So far, I have 100+ publications, an H-index of 30, 2,100+ citations, and 650+ verified peer reviews, along with open-source tools freely available to the community.

I am seeking an **Assistant Professorship** to build the **[Translational Computational Pharmacology (TCP) Group](https://shabanahmad.github.io/#research-vision)**. Full details on my research vision, publications, teaching, and CV are on my **[personal website](https://shabanahmad.github.io/)**.

> I contribute both through this account (`ShabanAhmad`) and through **[KU-MGB](https://github.com/KU-MGB)**, the Microbial Genomics and Biodegradation research group at PLEN, University of Copenhagen.
