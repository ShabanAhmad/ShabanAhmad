## About Me

I'm a computational biologist at **[PLEN, University of Copenhagen](https://plen.ku.dk/english/)**, building AI-driven pipelines at the intersection of structural biology, machine learning, and environmental biotechnology.

My path spans from **[Jamia Millia Islamia](https://www.jmi.ac.in/)** in New Delhi — where I earned my PhD developing deep-learning models for lung cancer drug discovery — through a visiting stint at the **[University of Jyväskylä, Finland](https://www.jyu.fi/en)**, to Copenhagen, where I now work on PFAS "forever chemicals". I have 100+ publications, an H-index of 30, 2,100+ citations, and 650+ verified peer reviews, alongside open-source tools anyone can run.

I am actively seeking an **Assistant Professorship** to establish the **[Translational Computational Pharmacology (TCP) Group](https://shabanahmad.github.io/#research-vision)**. Full details on my vision, publications, teaching, and CV are on my **[personal website →](https://shabanahmad.github.io/)**

> I commit both here (`ShabanAhmad`) and via **[KU-MGB](https://github.com/KU-MGB)** — the Microbial Genomics & Biodegradation research group at PLEN, University of Copenhagen.
